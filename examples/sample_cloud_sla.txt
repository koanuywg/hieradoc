ENTERPRISE CLOUD HOSTING & SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Document Code: CLOUD-SLA-2026

PART I: SERVICE COMMITMENTS AND AVAILABILITY
ARTICLE 1. UPTIME GUARANTEE AND SERVICE CREDIT
1. Provider guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95% for all production server clusters.
2. If Provider fails to meet this threshold, Customer shall be entitled to a Service Credit equal to 20% of the monthly fee for availability between 99.0% and 99.94%, and 50% of the monthly fee for availability below 99.0%.

ARTICLE 2. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE WINDOWS
1. Scheduled maintenance shall occur exclusively during off-peak hours (between 01:00 AM and 04:00 AM UTC on Sundays).
2. Provider must give at least 72 hours prior written notice before initiating any non-emergency scheduled maintenance.

PART II: DATA SECURITY AND BACKUP POLICIES
ARTICLE 3. AUTOMATED BACKUP AND DISASTER RECOVERY
1. Customer database snapshots are taken automatically every 6 hours and replicated across 3 geographic availability zones.
2. The Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is guaranteed at 6 hours, and Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is guaranteed at 2 hours.

ARTICLE 4. DATA RETENTION UPON TERMINATION
1. Upon contract expiration or termination, Customer data shall be preserved for 60 calendar days to allow full export.
2. After 60 days, all Customer production data will be permanently wiped using DoD 5220.22-M secure sanitization standards.
